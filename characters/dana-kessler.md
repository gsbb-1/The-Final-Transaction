# Dana Kessler

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Dana Kessler |
| Age | 44 |
| Gender | Female |
| Role | The physical threat |
| Job | Founder of a small private investigations and security firm in London; on retainer to Sebastian Crane |

## Profile

Kessler spent fifteen years in government intelligence work before leaving for the private sector, where the pay is better and nobody asks for a report. Her firm does due diligence, asset tracing and "problem resolution" for clients who value discretion. She is calm, observant and entirely professional. She is polite to the people she follows.

She does not hate Jonas or care about bonds. She has been asked to obtain a set of notebooks, and she will go up a ladder of methods one rung at a time: watch, search, offer, pressure, force. She dislikes the top rungs because they are messy, not because they are wrong.

## Wants

To finish the job cleanly and be paid. To never again work for an institution that can disown her.

## Fears and flaws

- She underestimates amateurs, and Jonas is an amateur.
- She has a rule about children that Crane has not been told about.
- She knows her client will deny her if it goes wrong.

## Relationships

- **Sebastian Crane:** client. She neither likes nor trusts him.
- **Her team:** two or three former colleagues who do the legwork.
- **Jonas:** the subject. She comes to respect him, which is inconvenient.

## Voice

Quiet, courteous, specific. Tells people exactly what will happen next, and then it does.

## Arc in act I

Surveillance, then a search of Jonas's house, then a direct offer, then a pursuit.
