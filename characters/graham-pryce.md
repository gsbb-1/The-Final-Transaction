# Graham Pryce

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Graham Pryce |
| Age | 57 |
| Gender | Male |
| Role | The one who learns the record exists (chapter 2) |
| Job | Chief risk officer, Walbrook Clearing House; Jonas's boss |

## Profile

Graham is a decent man three years from retirement who has spent a career learning which warnings an institution wants to hear. He is courteous, tired, and very good at the phrase "let's take that offline". He hired Jonas and likes him.

He is not a villain. He learns about the record by accident, is frightened by what it implies about his own failure to act, and does what he has always done with a problem: he passes it upward. That single act puts the record in play.

## Wants

To reach retirement with his pension and his reputation. For the crisis to happen on someone else's watch.

## Fears and flaws

- He signed off the reports that said everything was fine.
- He mistakes loyalty to the institution for doing the right thing.
- He knows Jonas was right three years ago, because he was the one who filed the memo.

## Relationships

- **Jonas:** protégé, and now a threat to him.
- **The Walbrook board:** he reports to them; one board member is close to Sebastian Crane.
- **The Treasury:** his contact there takes the news to Caroline Strand's office.

## Voice

Soft, hedged, managerial. Never says no; says "not yet".

## Arc in act I

From a man who can still tell himself he is managing a situation to one who has handed his own colleague to people he cannot control.

## Still to decide

- Whether he redeems himself later, and what it costs him.
