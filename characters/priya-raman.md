# Priya Raman

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Priya Raman |
| Age | 31 |
| Gender | Female |
| Role | Jonas's ally |
| Job | Senior analyst, collateral risk team, Walbrook Clearing House |

## Profile

Priya is the best analyst Jonas has worked with and the only person on his team who checks his work instead of trusting it. She has a mathematics degree, a fast temper, and a habit of saying aloud what a meeting is avoiding. She came to Walbrook four years ago and expected to stay two.

She does not know about the record. She knows that Jonas sees something in the data that he is not putting in the official reports, and it annoys her that he will not say what.

## Wants

To be taken seriously on her own account, not as Jonas's deputy. To be right in public.

## Fears and flaws

- Impatient with caution; she acts before she has cover.
- She has family money worries of her own: her parents' savings are in exactly the kind of fund that is about to be hit.
- She assumes that being right protects you.

## Relationships

- **Jonas:** mentor, and a source of frustration.
- **Graham Pryce:** she thinks he is a coward and has not hidden it well.

## Voice

Fast, exact, sarcastic. Says the number first and the explanation second.

## Arc in act I

She independently finds what Jonas has known for years, pulls the member report under her own login for him after his access is cut, runs the numbers for Monday's margin calls, and then stops answering her phone.

## Act II (decided 2026-10-09)

Detained Sunday noon on a Computer Misuse complaint about the Friday report, plus a written request from the Treasury; held about twenty hours; released Monday morning under investigation, phone and laptop kept, told not to contact anyone at Walbrook. Goes to her parents' in Leicester with no phone and surfaces Thursday through Calloway.

## Still to decide

- Whether she can be trusted completely, or has been approached by the other side.
