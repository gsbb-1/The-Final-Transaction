# Sebastian Crane

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Sebastian Crane |
| Age | 52 |
| Gender | Male |
| Role | Antagonist: the man who profits from the panic |
| Job | Founder and chief investment officer, Greywether Capital, a macro hedge fund in Mayfair |

## Profile

Crane made his name and his first fortune betting against a currency in an earlier crisis, and has spent the years since being told he was lucky. He is charming, well read, and patient. He gives to museums. He believes markets are a moral instrument: they punish those who lie to themselves, and he is only the messenger.

Greywether is positioned to make billions if British government bonds and the pound collapse. What Crane lacks is certainty about where the weakest points are and when they will break. Jonas's record is exactly that: a map, drawn over four years, of where the hidden leverage sits. In Crane's hands it turns a large bet into a sure one, and a panic into a rout.

## Wants

The record. To be proved right on a scale nobody can call luck.

## Fears and flaws

- Vanity: he needs the world to know it was him.
- He does not think of the people on the other side of his trades as people.
- He has convinced himself he is not responsible for what Dana Kessler does on his behalf.

## Relationships

- **Dana Kessler:** on retainer; he tells her the result he wants and nothing else.
- **A Walbrook board member:** an old friend, and his source inside the clearing house.
- **Caroline Strand:** he has donated to her party; she does not return his calls any more.
- **Khalid Al-Mansouri:** a former investor in Greywether who withdrew his money.

## Voice

Unhurried, amused, educated. Asks questions he already knows the answer to.

## Arc in act I

Hears that the record exists, decides he must have it, and moves from making an offer to making a threat.

## Still to decide

- Whether he only exploits the panic or had a hand in the event that triggered it.
- What he was to Jonas, if anything, in Jonas's trading years.
