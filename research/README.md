# Research

Background for the novel: how the real world works where the plot touches it. Notes are written for a novelist, not a practitioner: plain language, concrete detail usable in a scene, and a "plot implications" section that says what the manuscript should change. Each note ends with its sources. Anything unconfirmed is marked.

Rule from `AGENTS.md`: financial, legal and procedural detail must be accurate or deliberately fictional. When a note shows the manuscript stretches reality, record the choice (change it, or keep it and know why) in the decision log in `PLAN.md`.
