## For the author

- **The seventh-book entry** where the handwriting changes and Elsie alters the record is pointed at in chapters 29, 40, 50, 52 and 54 and never shown. Show it (chapter 40 is the place) or keep it as a deliberately withheld page.
- **The title phrase** "the final transaction" appears once, in chapter 36. Consider a second use.
- **Pace.** Chapters 15–19 and 29–33 are runs of people alone reading or deciding; chapters 36–41 are explanation and refusal; chapters 14 and 38 repeat the same Monday/Tuesday market set piece; chapters 54 and 55 overlapped (reduced). Candidates to cut: the Pell lunch in 18, half of 19, the second half of 24, chapter 33 (fold into 41).
- **Voice.** The Jonas chapters share tics: "hands flat" in twenty chapters, "I want that down" in eleven, "in the voice of a man" in thirteen.
- **Lengths.** About 112,600 words of prose; 35 chapters are over the 2,000-word guide; chapters 36–39 are the longest.
- **Unverified.** All finance, legal and police procedure beyond the research notes (Bank lending against a sovereign's purchases via clearing banks, a delayed market open, bail with a condition to assist the Bank, who executes the warrant on a hedge fund). 76 VERIFY comments remain.
- Jonas's disclosure to the Bank and Priya's emails are never answered on the page; the grey-jacket man who followed Kessler is never resolved.
